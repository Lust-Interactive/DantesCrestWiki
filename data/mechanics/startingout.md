When you start playing Dante's Crest, it can be helpful to understand the basics of the game mechanics and how to get started effectively. This section will guide you through the initial steps and provide tips for new players.

## Logging In
To begin your adventure, you will need to create an account and log in to the game. The current backdrop for the login scene contains [Frenemy Tower](../../locations/mysteria/#frenemy-tower), a notable location in Mysteria that seems to be fire.. or under seige.

## Character Creation
Once logged in, you will be prompted to create your character. There are very few customization options at this time, but you can choose your character's name. Make sure to pick a name that you like, as it will represent you in the game world. Other customization options are available and unlocked as you continue your journey.
